# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Ayame Editor contributors, maintainers, and users must help keep project spaces
welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment. Participation is open
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual
identity or orientation, or other protected characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberately intimidating conduct.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, release
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern to the repository owner by opening an
[issue](https://github.com/hjosugi/ayame-editor/issues). Do not include private
or sensitive details in a public issue; state only that private follow-up is
needed. The maintainer will review reports fairly and may remove content, issue
a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a
participant when necessary.

Good-faith reports will be handled without retaliation. Platform-level abuse
may also be reported through
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
